# HR-Analytics-Dashboard-Tableau
The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze employee workforce data and provide meaningful insights into employee attrition, demographics, departments, education, job satisfaction, and age groups.
The dashboard transforms raw HR data into an easy-to-understand visual report that can help HR teams and management identify workforce trends, understand employee attrition patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.
## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall employee workforce statistics.
- Understand employee attrition patterns.
- Compare attrition across different departments.
- Analyze attrition by gender.
- Understand attrition across different age groups.
- Analyze education field-wise attrition.
- Understand job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Compare active employees and employees who have left the organization.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making.

📊 Dashboard Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard contains multiple visualizations that provide an overall view of the organization's workforce.

Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides the following KPIs:

Employee Count
Attrition Count
Active Employees
Attrition Rate
Average Age

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the organization's current workforce and employee attrition situation.

📈 Dashboard Visualizations
1. Attrition by Gender

This visualization compares employee attrition between:

Female employees
Male employees

It helps identify whether attrition is concentrated more heavily within a particular gender group.

2. Attrition by Department

The department-level visualization analyzes employee attrition across:

Human Resources
Research & Development
Sales

This helps identify departments experiencing higher employee turnover.

3. Number of Employees by Age Group

The dashboard provides an age-based analysis of employees to understand workforce distribution across different age ranges.

Age groups include:

Under 25
25–34
35–44
45–54
Over 55

This analysis helps understand the age composition of the organization's workforce.

4. Attrition by Gender and Age Group

This visualization combines:

Gender
Age Group
Attrition

It uses donut charts to show the gender-wise attrition distribution across different age groups.

This helps identify specific demographic segments where employee attrition is higher.

5. Education Field-wise Attrition

The dashboard analyzes employee attrition based on education fields such as:

Life Sciences
Medical
Marketing
Technical Degree
Other

This helps understand whether employees from specific educational backgrounds have higher attrition.

6. Job Satisfaction Rating

The dashboard contains a job satisfaction matrix that analyzes satisfaction ratings across different job roles.

Job satisfaction is categorized from:

1 → Lowest Satisfaction
2
3
4 → Highest Satisfaction

The visualization helps identify job roles with relatively higher or lower satisfaction levels.

🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters that allow users to dynamically analyze the data.

Education Field

Users can filter the dashboard based on education field.

Department

Users can filter the dashboard based on department.

These filters allow users to drill down into specific employee groups and analyze their attrition patterns.
